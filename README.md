## Renewal terms: three different promises

The renewal clause decides whether a 11.11 price is a discount or a teaser, and providers handle it in three distinct ways.

**Same price forever.** EnderHost states plainly that the $11.11 annual price is recurring, and you keep paying it as long as the service stays active. LightLayer's 11.11 announcement makes the same commitment for its promotional products, listed as renewal at the same price with no increase.

**Fixed for a set number of years.** SmokyHosts promises its coupon pricing recurs for a minimum of three years. What happens in year four is not guaranteed, and the review coverage of that sale repeats the caveat.

**New purchases only.** OrangeVPS's 11.11 promotion doubles data transfer on every VPS plan and takes 11% off the BASIC-AP and BASIC-US lines, but the terms exclude existing services and renewals entirely. That sale runs 11–13 November on GMT+8, which makes today the last day.

For the standard LightLayer annual plans, the renewal price isn't published on the plan listing itself. Ask support for the renewal quote in writing before you commit to a year, and check whether the code you plan to use is recurring or first-term only.

## Where the deals bite

- **LightLayer limits you to two units per product** during the 11.11 promotion, and cancels unpaid orders after one hour, so have your payment method ready before you add anything to the cart.
- **LightLayer's refund policy covers provider faults only.** Regular-priced and promotional products are excluded otherwise, so a change of mind after deployment is not a refundable event.
- **SmokyHosts doesn't refund at all.** Its sale post says to clarify doubts before ordering, and deployment is manual, taking up to 12 hours after payment verification.
- **SmokyHosts' upgrades are opt-in and manual.** Bandwidth doubling, extra SSD and the storage bonus all require posting your order ID in the sale thread. Skip that and you get the base specification.
- **OrangeVPS's 11% discount covers two product lines only.** The doubled transfer applies across eligible VPS plans, but the percentage off doesn't reach VPS BUDGET or VDS.
- **RAKsmart allows one coupon per order**, with no stacking and no combining with reseller pricing, and flash-sale stock is capped at 20 units per product per day with no refunds. The page itself says final pricing is whatever the order total shows.
- **IPv4 isn't stated on LightLayer's plan listings.** Confirm the address allocation for your chosen location before paying if your workload needs inbound IPv4.

## Which tier fits

**Learning Linux or running a small personal site, cheapest possible:** a $11.11/year entry box is enough, and LightLayer's $24.99 annual plan is the upgrade if you want disk headroom and don't want to think about transfer limits.

**A public-facing site that needs IPv4:** skip the $11.11 band entirely. Budget $20–$32 a year, or take LightLayer's $24.99 annual plan after confirming the IP allocation.

**US routing with a choice of network path:** LightLayer's Los Angeles annual plans at $29.99, $39.99 and $49.99 are priced by route. Pick the route that matches where your traffic actually comes from, not the cheapest one.

**A 4-core box for a real application:** the mid-tier at roughly $11–18/month, or $53.60/month for 32 cores and 32GB.

**Heavy storage or a China-optimized line:** the 11.11 Hong Kong and Los Angeles configurations at $84 and $40 a month, while the 20 and 30 unit allocations last.

Before checkout, confirm three things: how many IPv4 addresses your plan includes, what the renewal price will be after the first term, and that the refund policy (provider fault only) is acceptable for what you're deploying.
